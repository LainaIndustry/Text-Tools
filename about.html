About WordCountPro

WordCountPro is a free collection of browser-based text tools designed for writers, students, editors, and anyone who works with words. The site includes a word counter, character counter, sentence counter, paragraph counter, case converter, text cleaner, keyword density checker, and reading time calculator.

Our Approach

Every tool on this site runs entirely in your browser. Your text is never uploaded to a server, never stored in a database, and never shared with third parties by us. This keeps the tools fast, private, and available even without an internet connection once the page has loaded.

Why We Built It

Many text tools online are cluttered with intrusive ads, require account creation, or upload your content to a server. WordCountPro was built as a clean, lightweight alternative that focuses on doing a few things well: accurate counting, useful statistics, and simple text transformations.

Technology

The site is built with plain HTML, CSS, and vanilla JavaScript. There is no backend, no database, and no build step. This makes it fast to load and easy to host on any static hosting platform.

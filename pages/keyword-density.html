What Is Keyword Density?

Keyword density is the percentage of times a word appears in a text relative to the total number of words. For example, if a word appears 10 times in a 500-word article, its density is 2%. This tool reports density using the total word count including stop words, which matches common practice.

How to Use This Tool

Paste your text into the editor. The table updates automatically and shows the most frequent words. Very common words (like "the", "and", "of") are excluded because they appear in almost every text and are not useful as keywords.

This tool does not make SEO ranking claims. Keyword density is only one small signal among many used by search engines.

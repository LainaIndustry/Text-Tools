How Paragraphs Are Counted

This tool splits your text on blank lines (two or more newline characters with optional spaces between them). Each non-empty block counts as one paragraph. Single line breaks within a block are treated as part of the same paragraph.

Why Paragraph Count Matters

Paragraphs help structure writing and improve readability. Short, focused paragraphs are generally easier to scan on screen. Blog posts, news articles, and web copy often use shorter paragraphs than print books or academic papers.

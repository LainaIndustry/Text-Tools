Case Conversion Options

            
    
                
  • UPPERCASE: every letter is converted to a capital letter.
    • 
                
  • lowercase: every letter is converted to a lowercase letter.
    • 
                
  • Title Case: the first letter of every word is capitalized, and the rest is lowercase.
    • 
                
  • Sentence case: the first letter of each sentence is capitalized, and the rest is lowercase.
    • 
            

            
When to Use a Case Converter

            
Case converters are useful when you paste text that has inconsistent casing, when you want to normalize headings, or when you need to format text for a specific style. They save time compared with retyping.

How Sentences Are Counted

The tool detects sentence boundaries using the three standard sentence-ending marks: periods (.), question marks (?), and exclamation points (!). Repeated marks such as "!!!" are treated as a single sentence ending. If your text contains content but no ending punctuation, it is counted as one sentence.

Why Sentence Length Matters

Average sentence length is one indicator of readability. Very long sentences can be harder to follow, while a healthy mix of short and medium sentences often reads more naturally. Many style guides suggest aiming for an average of 15–20 words per sentence for general audiences.

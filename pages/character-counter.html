What Is a Character Counter?

A character counter reports how many individual characters a piece of text contains. This includes letters, numbers, spaces, punctuation, and line breaks. Many platforms — social networks, meta descriptions, SMS messages — have character limits, so a character counter helps you stay within them.

Characters With and Without Spaces

Some systems count every character including spaces, while others ignore whitespace. This tool shows both numbers so you can match whichever rule you are writing for.

Why Character Count Matters

  • Search engines often display only about 155–160 characters of a meta description.
  • Social platforms limit posts and bios by character count.
  • Print and design layouts may require text to fit a fixed character budget.

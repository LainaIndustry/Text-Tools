How Reading Time Is Estimated

Reading time is calculated by dividing the word count by an average reading speed. This tool uses 225 words per minute, which is within the commonly cited range of 200–250 words per minute for adult readers of English.

Speaking time uses 130 words per minute, a reasonable pace for clear presentations and speeches. Actual times vary by reader, topic complexity, and presentation style.

Reading Level Estimate

The reading level label is a rough estimate based on a simplified readability formula. It is intended as a general indicator, not a formal assessment. For precise readability scores, use dedicated readability tools.

What Does a Text Cleaner Do?

            
A text cleaner removes formatting artifacts that often appear when text is copied from emails, PDFs, or web pages. Common issues include double spaces between words, tabs mixed with spaces, and multiple blank lines between paragraphs.

            
Cleaning Options

            
    
                
  • Remove Extra Spaces: collapses repeated spaces and tabs into a single space and trims spaces around line breaks.
    • 
                
  • Remove Blank Lines: reduces multiple consecutive blank lines to a single blank line.
    • 
                
  • Clean Everything: applies both operations in one step.

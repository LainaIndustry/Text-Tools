We welcome feedback, questions, and business inquiries. Because WordCountPro is a static site with no backend, we do not operate a contact form that submits directly. Please use the email addresses below.

About Contact Forms

If you wish to add a contact form to this site, you can connect it to an external form service such as Formspree, Netlify Forms, or Google Forms. The form action URL in the HTML would need to be replaced with your service endpoint. This site intentionally ships without a form so that no submissions are lost or misrepresented.

Response Time

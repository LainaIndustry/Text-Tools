Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how WordCountPro ("we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our website and tools.

Text You Enter

All text you enter into our tools is processed locally in your browser. We do not upload, transmit, store, or have access to the text you enter. The tools work entirely on your device using JavaScript.

Personal Information

We do not require account registration and do not collect names, email addresses, or other personal information through the tools themselves. If you contact us by email, we will receive the information you choose to include in that message.

Cookies and Local Storage

We may use your browser's local storage to remember your theme preference (light or dark mode). This preference is stored on your device and is not transmitted to us.

Third-party services such as Google AdSense may use cookies to display relevant advertisements. These cookies are controlled by the third-party provider, not by us. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for details on how Google handles data.

Advertising

This site may display advertisements served by Google AdSense. AdSense may use cookies and similar technologies to show ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can learn more about Google's advertising practices and opt-out options by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

Analytics

If analytics services are added to this site in the future, they may collect anonymized usage data such as page views and general geographic region. This policy will be updated to reflect any such services.

Children's Privacy

Our tools are not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us through our contact page.

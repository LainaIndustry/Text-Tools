Cookie Policy

Last updated: January 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how WordCountPro uses cookies and similar technologies such as local storage.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device by your web browser. They are widely used to remember preferences and to help websites function properly.

How We Use Cookies and Local Storage

We use browser local storage to remember your theme preference (light or dark mode). This preference is stored on your device and is not shared with us.

We do not set first-party tracking cookies.

Third-Party Cookies

If Google AdSense advertisements are displayed on this site, Google and its partners may set cookies to serve relevant ads. These cookies are controlled by Google, not by us. You can manage your Google ad settings through Google's Ads Settings page.

Managing Cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect how some parts of the site work, such as the theme preference.

Changes

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page.

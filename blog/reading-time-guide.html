Reading Time: How Long Does It Take to Read an Article?

ReadingFebruary 10, 20265 min read

Introduction

Reading time estimates help readers decide whether to start an article now or save it for later. They are also useful for writers who want to understand the pacing of their content. This guide explains how reading time is calculated and what the numbers mean.

Average Reading Speed

Research and common practice place average adult reading speed for English at roughly 200 to 250 words per minute. This varies widely by individual, topic difficulty, and reading purpose. A familiar novel reads faster than a technical manual.

This site uses 225 words per minute as its default reading speed — a middle value within the commonly cited range.

Speaking Time

Speaking time is usually slower than reading time because speakers pause, emphasize, and breathe. A common estimate for clear presentation delivery is around 130 words per minute. This site uses 130 words per minute for speaking time estimates.

How Reading Time Is Calculated

The formula is simple: word count divided by words per minute. For example, a 1,000-word article at 225 words per minute takes about 4.4 minutes to read. The tool rounds to a friendly display such as "4 min" or "< 1 min" for very short texts.

Using Reading Time Estimates

  • Show reading time on blog posts so readers can plan.
  • Estimate speaking time for scripts, speeches, and videos.
  • Compare lengths across articles in a series.

Try our reading time calculator to estimate reading and speaking time for your own text.

Conclusion

Reading time estimates are approximations, not exact predictions. They are most useful for relative comparisons and for giving readers a sense of what to expect. Use them alongside word count and other metrics.
